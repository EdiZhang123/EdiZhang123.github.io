I just got back from Mesa, Arizona for College Club Swimming Nationals today (6 hour drive) ! Nationals was so fun and I had a great time making friends and competing once again. My only individual event, the 200 IM was okay-I went slower than last year but still not too bad. My 200 free and 100 free relay splits weren't so great either-both slower than last year, but still really fun to swim with the other guys. The most fun was the 4x50 Free relay, where we made finals and won the C final. I had a cut on the bottom of my foot from doing a turn that made my foot kind of hurt, but nevertheless I swam pretty well (22.49 in prelims, 21.70 in finals). Having basically never sprinted in high school, this was super fun to be apart of.
