This is an experience I will probably be dragging for years to come, because I've learned so much from just a few days of traveling. I went to Banff, Canada for four days with seven other people, 6 of which I've never met before, and I had the most fun time of my life. In the last nine months, I've had <a href="/posts/2026/01/blog-post-11/">no shortage of traveling</a>, and I can safely say this might have been the single best trip I went on.

I spent the first two days skiing at Lake Louise and Sunshine Village, then went on the famous Gondola Tour followed by a beautiful hike (I forgot the peak name).

**Being decisive and committing**
When my friend told me there was one spot open, I decided that day I was going to go, and bought my ticket. I was glad I made that quick decision and followed through with it instead of dilly-dallying in my head. For a moment I was debating, and ultimately felt like I simply had nothing to lose from going on this trip, and I think this is a mindset I am trying to adopt towards more things especially since what could possibly be the consequence for a 20-year-old other than having more colorful life stories and adventures.

**Making friends with strangers**
I basically only knew one person out of the other seven, which could easily be intimidating. Instead, we quickly bonded, and it felt super easy to connect with everyone. We even plan on playing intramural sports together now!
