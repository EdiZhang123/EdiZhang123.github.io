UCLA is the most beautiful campus ever, and I love the fact that all residential buildings are together. The food as delicious, and the people are super nice. I can't say my first quarter was a crazy experience, but it was certainly super comprehensive. I've never been so overstimulated with people everywhere, but I definitely love it. I honestly kind of forgot what happened this quarter.

**Classes**

MATH 33A: Love Pablo with all my heart. Took this class with Kimi, Shawn, Fiona, and Jeff. I missed this 8 am like three times the whole quarter. Adam Zheleznyak was an insanely good TA, and I went to a lot of his office hours. Was challenging at first, but became pretty smooth after Week 3.

CHEM 20A: Did not enjoy this class, but did learn some quantum mechanics. I didn't take chemistry at all in high school, so this definitely wasn't super easy.

ASTR 5: My professor was Neil DeGrasse Tyson's PhD advisor, which was the coolest thing ever.

HIST 9D: Learned about the history of the Middle East and Islam from a famous professor, James Gelvin. The Israel-Hamas war started a few weeks into the class, so I feel like this was a really valuable class to learn more about the background of current events.

**Overall**: Kind of a surreal experience. My room has the best view on the Hill.
