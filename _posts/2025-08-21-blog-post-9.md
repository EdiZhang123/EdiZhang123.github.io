I'm currently on my flight home from Barcelona after a long ten days in Europe. This summer has been extremely fruitful, and I still have about five days in NYC before I go back home to the bay. In these few days, I'm visiting friends from middle and high school and seeing the Great Gatsby on Broadway!

Highlights from the summer:

**Completed my SWE Internship at Capital One**

- As a late start intern, it was a little bit intimidating since we had no structured onboarding like the rest of the interns, but I felt more comfortable since two other interns on my team were also late start. We swarmed our work the first few weeks as we all felt a little lost, thus we wanted to work together.

- I worked on a team called Well Managed Central, which is under Card Tech+. It is an internal platform with about 4600 users weekly that provides organized summary metrics of all sorts of data for software used by Card Tech+, such as security data scans for example.

- Our project was to automate batch processing of configuration compliance data from an external database (which previously was just being exported quarterly in an excel sheet)into Well Managed Central, consisting of user-friendly graphics, tools, and resources. Essentially, all software that Capital One uses must satisfy configuration criteria and compliance thresholds, and thus having a centralized platform to easily access your compliance information is a huge plus for developer productivity.

- I worked on front-end designing to create the UI for users that wish to view their compliance data, simulate changes, and access resources for assistance. I used React, Typescript, and GraphQL for this. I picked up the tech stack pretty quickly, and it was a great learning experience.

- We developed this Micro-frontend and completed test coverage just in time for our final presentation, which we presented to Engineering 360.

- Our intern team had a lot of fun together! We traveled to many cities and did fun activities like white water rafting, bowling, and biking

**Experience of living on my own**

I was lucky enough to be provided corporate housing by Capital One, which pretty much dealt with all my housing issues for me by giving me an amazing fully-furnished spacious apartment. Living by myself, I definitely think I grew up a lot this summer

- While I spent weekends traveling, I cooked dinner for myself every day that I was in Richmond, and I think my cooking skills and speed have definitely improved

- I severely underestimated how my grocery needs

- I traveled somewhere every weekend to experience something new, and I was able to make spontaneous plans involving many steps and follow through on them. When my intern team and I planned our trip to Cape Cod, both of our flights got cancelled the day of due to storms. On the spot, I thought of a 5-part solution involving taking the early morning company shuttle up to McLean HQs, taking DC Metro to DCA, flying to Boston, booking a bus to Hyannis, and then taking an Uber to the Beach House in West Harwich.

- Developing agency and taking charge of everything I am doing, being less passive

- Planning things out many steps ahead and accounting for small details and potential itinerary disruptions

- Learning to be highly flexible and portable

- I severely took California weather for granted
