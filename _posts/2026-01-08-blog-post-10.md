Flying on the A380 back to LAX from Seoul-Incheon, it's finally occurred to me how fast the past year has gone by, but on the contrary, January 2025 feels so long ago. This past year, I've learned so much, whether it be academically, professionally, and socially. I've also done a ton of traveling which has helped develop me into who I am today. I can feel my independence and living skills growing exponentially, and I am on a completely different level than I was a year ago.

Highlights since the start summer:

**Completed my SWE Internship at Capital One**

First experience in a corporate environment, working on existing codebases, navigating how to work with a large team, understanding how to make the most out of an experience and figuring out what is actually valuable.

**Experience of living on my own**

Being independent and working through issues on my own, developing confidence in my problem-solving abilities.

**My travel map this summer**

A large part of my last year consisted of <a href="/posts/2026/01/blog-post-11/">traveling</a>. I feel like this has made me a much more brave and confident person, as well as someone with more adventurous spirit.

Overall this was definitely the most eventful transformative year of my life. I've started to find my place in college and search within myself to discover what I really love and what kind of people I enjoy being around. In one condensed year, I've experienced and learned so much from friendships, relationships, and uncomfortable situations that I've always navigated through successfully.
