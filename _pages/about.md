I am an undergraduate studying applied mathematics with a focus in electrical engineering and computer science at the University of California-Los Angeles.

Last summer I was a Software Engineering Intern at Capital One. Before that, I was a researcher at the Cleveland Clinic Main Campus in the <a href="https://www.lerner.ccf.org/musculoskeletal-imaging/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Advanced Musculoskeletal Imaging Lab</a> under <a href="https://www.lerner.ccf.org/biomedical-engineering/li/#lab-investigator" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Xiaojuan Li</a>, where I researched <a href="https://github.com/EdiZhang123/Bone-Shape-Modeling" target="_blank">Bone Shape Modeling</a> and presented my work at the 2025 <a href="https://www.ors.org/" target="_blank">Orthopaedic Research Society</a> Annual Conference in Phoenix, Arizona. My interests broadly lie in applications of mathematical modeling, software engineering, and machine learning.
